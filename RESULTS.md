## Defects found and fixed during development

The suites caught these before publication, not after:

1. `plan` originally planned an absolute order-statistic count, which *smaller*
   runs satisfy more easily; replaced with a fractional rank window, which is
   monotone in the right direction.
2. The CSV header heuristic matched `timestamp` via `time` and read the wrong
   column; replaced with a priority list that tries latency-shaped names first.
3. Order-statistic coverage could land below the nominal level for some
   percentile/sample-size combinations (e.g. 94.35% for p99 at n=1200); ranks are
   now widened until the reported coverage reaches the requested confidence.
4. Three test expectations were wrong (an incomplete-beta value, `P(X≤0)` for a
   binomial, and the direction of the small-sample bound) and were corrected
   against the definitions rather than adjusted to match the code.
